Before you enter

A few clear answers.

Am I trading real money?

No. Every account balance, position, and result in SimuTrade Labs uses simulated funds. The app does not connect to a brokerage or execute real orders.

Is the market feed live?

The included feed is synthetic practice data and is labeled throughout the Lab. The backend includes a Twelve Data connector for compatible symbols when a licensed API key is configured. Coverage, delay, and instrument access depend on the provider. No live futures or options entitlement is included.

Will this help me pass a prop firm challenge?

You can practice position sizing, drawdown awareness, and consistent decision-making against editable rules. No result or qualification is guaranteed. Confirm each firm’s current rules directly before configuring your profile.

Do I need an account?

You can practice as a guest with progress stored in this browser. Create an account to save your work on the server and access it after signing in on another device. Guest progress stays separate.

What is available in the operator workspace?

A contact pipeline, saved drafts, offer and content frameworks, a research planner, and a follow-up planning queue. Optional Gemini generation requires a server-side API key. Email sending, booking, paid subscriptions, and outside CRM synchronization are not connected.
