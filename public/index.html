A little clarity

Before you enter.

Is any real money involved?+

All balances, positions, and results are simulated. There is no broker connection, deposit, or real-money execution. Simulation results do not predict live trading results.

Is the market data live?+

The included feed is synthetic practice data, labeled in the Lab. An optional external provider connector requires configured credentials and suitable data entitlements. Live market data is not included in this build.

Can I prepare for a prop firm challenge?+

You can rehearse custom targets, fixed balance-based daily and overall loss limits, risk caps, and active days. Verify your provider’s rules first. Trailing equity drawdown, payout eligibility, and firm-specific enforcement are not modeled. Passing is never promised.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Yes. The browser workspace adapts to mobile screens, with touch controls and collapsible navigation. No app-store download is needed. Keep a copy of important notes using the built-in exports.

Do stops and targets close my positions automatically?+

No. They are planning references in this version. You close simulated positions manually at the current quote. Fees, slippage, margin, and intrabar execution are not modeled.
