Terms & simulation limits

Educational simulation

SimuTrade Labs is an educational practice workspace. All funds, positions, and trade results are simulated. There is no broker connection or real order execution. Nothing in the app is a financial recommendation or a guarantee of profits, qualifications, or funded-account access.

Data and execution limits

The included market feed is synthetic and is labeled as such. Optional provider quotes depend on licensing, coverage, timestamps, and delays. Entries and exits use observed quotes. Stop and target inputs are planning references only and do not automatically execute. Fees, slippage, margin, and realistic intrabar fill mechanics are excluded.

Challenge and coaching limits

Challenge profiles are generic and editable. This build measures fixed overall loss and realized daily loss against starting balance, with UTC day boundaries. It does not enforce trailing equity drawdown, payout criteria, consistency rules, news restrictions, or other provider-specific conditions. The built-in coach uses disclosed rules, not psychological diagnosis. AI and template drafts require human review.

Operator features

The contact pipeline, content tools, and follow-up planner save working records. Email delivery, booking, billing, background automations, and external CRM sync are not configured. No provider affiliation is implied. Use this workspace only if you are 18 or older. Verify applicable product terms before public launch.
