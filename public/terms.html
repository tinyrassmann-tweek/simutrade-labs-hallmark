Evaluation practice and simulation terms

Updated November 20, 2026.

Evaluation preparation

SimuTrade Labs provides practice for students preparing for prop-firm evaluations. Study rules, rehearse a simulated session, and review how consistently you followed your plan. Passing an evaluation is a learning goal. Your provider decides its own evaluation and funding outcomes.

Simulated funds and market replay

All positions and balances use simulated funds. The included feed is synthetic replay. There is no broker execution. Market entries and manual exits use the displayed practice quotes. Stop and target inputs are planning references and require manual exits. Fees, slippage, margin, and intrabar fill mechanics are excluded.

Challenge rules

Practice profiles use editable targets, fixed overall loss, realized daily loss against starting balance, minimum active days, and per-trade risk limits. Days reset in UTC. Trailing equity drawdown, news restrictions, consistency caps, payout eligibility, and firm-specific conditions require separate study against your provider’s current rulebook.

Coaching and study groups

The discipline coach uses software rules and structured reflection prompts. The study track is self-guided. Team notes support cohort study. Use your own evaluation rules and judgment when translating practice into an actual evaluation. No provider affiliation, funded-account qualification, profit, or pass is promised.

Accounts and use

You must be 18 or older to create an account or purchase. Keep your credentials private and use one account for each learner. Follow the cohort order terms for paid access. Your original journal content remains yours to export. The operator tools save drafts and planning records for you to review before use.
