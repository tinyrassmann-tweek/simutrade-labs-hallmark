Cohort order terms

Updated November 20, 2026. Order terms version simutrade-cohort-2026-11-20.

Your team subscription

The Founding Cohort costs $19 USD per month for six monthly billing periods. That total includes up to five seats, including the team owner. The founding subscription ends after its six-month term. Premium costs $49 USD per month and includes up to ten seats, including the owner. Premium renews monthly until cancellation. Any applicable taxes are shown at checkout.

You may request an optional Premium upgrade during the founding term. The director confirms the change, billing timing, and updated seat access with you before it takes effect.

What is included

Each learner has a private saved evaluation practice workspace, simulated trades, a journal, a study track, and rules-based coaching prompts. The team has a shared study-note area and invitation management. Seat limits include active members and unexpired reserved invitations. Invitations expire after seven days. Team members see notes deliberately shared with the team. Individual journals remain private.

The included experience is self-guided study with software coaching prompts. It uses synthetic replay and generic editable challenge profiles. Current practice limits are described in the simulation terms.

Payment and activation

Payments are processed by Think Tank Digital. Your receipt or card statement may show Think Tank Digital. Create and verify your learner account, create your team, accept these terms, and continue to the existing checkout. The director verifies payment and the subscription, then activates paid team access within one business day. Keep your receipt if support needs to match an order to your account. Free guest practice is available while activation is pending.

Cancellation and support

Submit a cancellation request through billing support before the next renewal. The director processes requests within one business day and confirms the effective date. Paid access normally continues through the paid billing period. The Founding Cohort ends after six billing periods unless you choose an upgrade. Export the records you want to keep.

Contact support if access was never activated, an activation deadline was missed, or a service problem prevents use. We will restore access or refund the unprovided service period. Applicable customer rights continue to apply.

License and use

Think Tank Solutions AI provides access for the learners occupying your included seats. Use separate accounts for each learner. Your own journal entries and study notes remain yours to export and share. Use the service for education, respectful cohort study, and lawful purposes. You must be 18 or older to create an account or purchase access.
