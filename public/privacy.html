Privacy notice

Updated November 20, 2026.

What we collect

Think Tank Solutions AI operates SimuTrade Labs. Account access uses Google Firebase Authentication for email verification and password recovery. SimuTrade stores your name, email, private practice records, session records, accepted order-terms version, team membership, invitations, and study notes you deliberately share. Guest practice is stored in your browser.

Think Tank Digital and Stripe process payments and billing records. SimuTrade receives an order reference, plan, verified activation status, and paid access dates from its operator. The app does not store payment card details. Inquiry and support forms store the contact information, message, consent, and submission reference you supply.

How information is used

Information supports your account, saved work, team invitations, customer support, billing reconciliation, and the features you select. Your team sees its member names, roles, and shared study notes. Your private journal and simulated trading history are available through your own account. Share personal information in a team note only when you want your teammates to see it.

Storage and service providers

Hosted account and workspace records use Google Cloud and Firestore. The venture director uses Grok-assisted operations to handle inquiries, verify Think Tank Digital orders, manage activation, and review service health. Automated marketing messages are separate from account and service communications. The current free practice feed is synthetic, and software coaching uses disclosed rules.

Retention and your choices

Keep your account records while you use the service and export your workspace from Settings whenever you want a copy. Sessions expire after seven days. Invitations expire after seven days. Billing records remain in the payment processor under its retention requirements. To request access, correction, deletion, account closure, or inquiry removal, use the support form. The director verifies control of the account or order before disclosing or deleting records. Records under an unresolved billing or legal hold are reviewed separately.

This release uses no advertising trackers. Signing up for product updates is optional. Contact privacy support to withdraw a product-update request.
